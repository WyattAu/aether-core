# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all contributors and participants in the Project Aether community, including but not limited to: maintainers, reviewers, issue filers, discussion participants, and anyone interacting with the project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and related communication channels (both public and private).

## Standards

Expected behavior includes:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Providing and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes and correcting them promptly
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language or actions
- Personal or political attacks directed at other participants
- Publishing private information (such as addresses or contact details) without explicit consent
- Destructive behavior including trolling, spamming, or deliberate disruption of discussions
- Sustained disruption of project workflows, including repeated nit-picking after a decision has been made

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers by contacting the project team through the private reporting channels outlined in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or by opening a confidential email to the project maintainers.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainers reserve the right to take action they deem appropriate, up to and including permanent exclusion from the community, for any pattern of behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Project maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face removal from the project leadership.

## Contact

For questions about this Code of Conduct, please open an issue with the `code-of-conduct` label or contact the project maintainers directly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), version 2.1.
